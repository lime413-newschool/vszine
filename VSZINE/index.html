Editors' letter

Dear Angels,

Some of us grew up flipping through the catalog when it landed in the mailbox. Some of us only know the show from YouTube clips and Tumblr reblogs. Either way, the wings, the glitter and the pink stripes are part of how a lot of girls learned what "pretty" was supposed to look like.

This issue covers the 2026 show, looks back at where it came from, and asks what we want to keep from that version of girlhood.

xoxo, the editors

02 · ANGEL AURA
History

A short history of the Angels

  1. 1977

    Roy Raymond opens the first store at the Stanford Shopping Center in California, pitched as a lingerie shop where men would feel comfortable buying gifts.

  2. 1982

    Leslie Wexner's company, The Limited, buys the brand. The mail-order catalog becomes its main way into people's homes.

  3. 1995

    The first fashion show is held at the Plaza Hotel in New York.

  4. 1997

    An ad campaign calls five models "Angels," and the name sticks.

  5. 2001

    The show is broadcast on network TV for the first time. Wings, Fantasy Bras and pop-star performers become the formula.

  6. 2018

    Ratings fall. A top executive says trans and plus-size models don't fit the show's "fantasy." He apologizes after public backlash.

  7. 2019

    The show is cancelled. In 2021 the brand retires the Angels and launches a new group of spokeswomen.

  8. 2024

    The runway returns in New York, wings and Angels included. A second New York show follows in 2025.

  9. 2026

    The show moves to Los Angeles for its third edition since the comeback.

06 · LOOKBOOK
The Looks

Color stories

Lime

The show opened and closed its first act on green. Bella Hadid wore a lime pareo under beaded flower wings, and Adriana Lima came back for her 21st year in a lime lace bodysuit.

Cobalt & turquoise

Joan Smalls brought a beachy cobalt set with curved wings. Awar Odhiang wore turquoise with bright blue feathered wings that spiraled around her like fireworks.

Gold

Candice Swanepoel shone in a gold look, one of several she wore that night. Alex Consani paired gold wings with black lace.

Barbie pink

Barbara Palvin walked in bright pink just a month after having her first baby.

White

Two big entrances in white: Kylie Jenner's surprise walk in feathered wings, and Adriana Lima's finale.

Snowball

Mathilda Gvarliani's mini dress, dotted with little pom-poms, was the most playful piece of the night.

10 · ORDER FORM
Girlhood
What does it mean to be a good girl?

For a long time the catalog sold one picture of a woman: very thin, mostly white, always smiling, always available. Girls saw it before they were old enough to buy anything from it.

In 2018 an executive told Vogue the show was a "fantasy" that didn't include trans or plus-size models. People pushed back, ratings kept falling, and the show disappeared for five years.

The 2026 runway looked different. Alex Consani, Paloma Elsesser, Ashley Graham and Precious Lee walked alongside the 2000s Angels. Six Black women wore wings.

Critics say this is a rebrand selling nostalgia back to the same girls it once made feel small. Fans say the glitter, the wings and the sleepover feeling are worth taking back on their own terms.

Which part of the pink-striped girlhood do you want to keep, and which part should stay in 2005?
